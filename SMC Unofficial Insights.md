## TÁCTICAS NO OFICIALES - META ADS

### Audience Segmentation (Granular)
Crear múltiples ad sets por micro-segmento:
- **By Income**: Top 10%, Top 5%, Ultra High Net Worth
- **By Behavior**: Multiple property owners (lookalike de propios clientes)
- **By Interest Stack**: Luxury + Travel + Real Estate + Finance
- **By Geography**: Miami neighborhoods (Brickell, Wynwood, Midtown) + International (Brazil, Argentina, Canada)
- **By Intent**: Custom audience de sitio web + product viewers

**Insight**: Micro-segmentation vs. broad audience = 30-40% mejor ROAS, aunque volumen menor.

### Creative Fatigue Management
- **Rotation**: Cambiar creativo cada 5-7 días si CPM sube >20%
- **Frequency Cap**: Máx 3 vistas/día por usuario (evita ad fatigue)
- **Ad Relevance Score**: Parar ads <5 puntos inmediatamente
- **Creative Refresh**: Cada 2 semanas generar 3-5 variaciones nuevas

**Tactic**: Tener 10-15 creatives en rotación simultáneamente para luxury = mejor rendimiento que 2-3 creatives estáticos.

### Auction Rules & Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO)
- **CBO**: Distribuye presupuesto automáticamente entre ad sets según rendimiento
- **Advantage**: 15-20% mejor eficiencia vs. manual ad set budgets
- **Pitfall**: Requiere 15+ conversiones diarias para entrenar bien
- **New Campaign**: Primeros 7-10 días monitoreo diario, luego optimiza automático

### Conversion Window Optimization
- **Default**: 28 días (view) + 1 día (click)
- **Realidad Real Estate**: Considera 60-90 días (lead → conversión real = meses)
- **Tactic**: Usa Conversions API con custom events para journey completo

### Dynamic Ads + Catalog
- **Setup**: Sube catálogo de propiedades (nombre, imagen, descripción, URL)
- **Retargeting**: Mostrar propiedad exacta que el usuario vio
- **Insight**: Yield 2-3x vs. static ads en retargeting luxury

### Landing Page Optimization
- **Load Speed**: <2 segundos (mobile) = +25% conversión
- **Mobile-First**: 70%+ tráfico es mobile en real estate
- **Video Hero**: Propiedad vista desde dron/tour 3D = +40% engagement
- **Form Friction**: Progressive profiling (1-2 campos primero, más después)

## TÁCTICAS NO OFICIALES - GOOGLE ADS

### Keyword Strategy (Search)
- **Mix**: 40% branded, 40% high-intent non-branded, 20% exploratory
- **High-Intent Keywords**: "buy luxury apartment Miami", "waterfront penthouse", "investment property Miami"
- **Competitor Bidding**: Bid on competitor branded + "alternative" terms
- **Long-Tail**: "2-bed luxury condo Brickell with pool under $1M" = lower CPC, higher conversion

### Quality Score Levers
- **CTR**: Mejorar titulo/descripción → A/B test 5+ variaciones
- **Landing Page**: Match keyword intent 100% (if keyword is "penthouse", LP debe ser penthouse, no generic)
- **Ad Relevance**: No usar 20 keywords en 1 ad group, máx 5-8

**Tactic**: Estructura en micro ad groups (1-3 keywords relacionadas) = QS +2-3 puntos.

### Smart Bidding (Target CPA/ROAS)
- **Ramp Time**: 2 semanas para entrenar
- **Minimum Data**: 15-30 conversiones/semana requeridas
- **ROAS Strategy**: Opción superior si tienes datos, 20-30% mejor ROI
- **Danger**: Cambiar settings mientras entrena = resetea learning

### Display Network Secrets
- **Exclusions**: Excluir competitors, low-quality publishers
- **Topic Targeting**: Real Estate + Investment + Luxury Goods
- **Placement**: Manuales a sites premium (Bloomberg, WSJ, Luxury magazine sites)
- **Remarketing**: Separar por audience behavior (product viewers vs. cart abandoners)

### YouTube Advertising Strategy
- **In-Stream (Skippable)**: Mejor para awareness, cheap ($0.15-0.30 CPV)
- **Non-Skippable Bumper**: 6 seg, high impact, $0.25-0.50 CPV, conversion low
- **Discovery Ads**: Thumbnail + title, usuario busca real estate = mejor intent
- **Placement**: Colocar en canales relacionados (architecture, travel, finance)

**Insight**: YouTube In-Stream remarketing a website visitors = highest ROI (0.1-0.2 CPV, 3-5% CTR).

## CAC REDUCTION TACTICS

### Lead Quality Scoring
- **Cheap Leads**: Anyone fills form = baja calidad
- **Qualified Leads**: Pre-screen en form (mínimo $1M+ budget, serio intent)
- **Tactic**: Usa conditional form fields: "Are you looking to purchase in next 3-6 months?" Si No → escalate to nurture track

### Content-Led Approach (Top Funnel)
- **TOFU**: Buyer guides, market reports, neighborhood guides (free, gated)
- **MOFU**: Property comparisons, investment calculators
- **BOFU**: Consultation booking, private showings

**Insight**: TOFU content ads = 70% lower CAC, pero lead quality baja. Mix ratio: 50% TOFU, 30% MOFU, 20% BOFU spend.

### Email List Building (Email = Higher LTV)
- **Gate valuable content**: "Miami Luxury Real Estate Report 2024" → email required
- **Email Retargeting**: 5-7 email nurture, después lanzar ads remarketing
- **Email List Monetization**: CPA via email = 3-5x lower que cold outreach

### CRM Integration
- **Conversions API**: Envia CRM data back a Meta (actual sale, timeline, etc)
- **Lead Scoring**: Mark hot/warm/cold in CRM → Meta optimizes accordingly
- **Closed-loop Reporting**: Track ad → lead → client → revenue (proves ROI beyond platform)

## TESTING FRAMEWORKS

### A/B Testing Cadence
- **Creative**: Test 2 new creatives/week (rotation)
- **Copy**: Test 2-3 headlines/descriptions simultaneously
- **Audience**: Test 1 new micro-segment/week
- **Offer**: Test booking vs. information request vs. price inquiry
- **Timing**: Run test minimum 7-14 days before concluding

### What Wins in Luxury Real Estate Ads
1. **Aerial/Drone footage**: +35% engagement vs. standard photo
2. **Lifestyle shots (not just property)**: Pool, sunset, entertaining = +25%
3. **Problem-Solution copy**: "Tired of viewing cookie-cutter apartments?" = +40%
4. **Social proof**: "Sold in 48 hours", "Owned by [celebrity]" = +20%
5. **Urgency (real)**: "3 units left" = +15%
6. **Price transparency**: Listing price = +10-15% CTR (no hidden fees)

### Creatives That Underperform
- Generic "luxury" text on image
- Too much text on creative (hard to read mobile)
- Grainy/low-res photos
- Overly salesy tone ("Act now!", "Limited time!!!!")
- Before/after heavy filtering (luxury audience suspicious)

## BUDGET & PACING STRATEGY

### Minimum Effective Spend
- **Meta**: $5-10/day per ad set to generate sufficient data (14+ days)
- **Google Search**: $10-20/day minimum for meaningful data
- **Daily Budget**: Never less than $500 total daily spend across campaign (insufficient signal)

### Seasonal Variations
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Higher demand, higher CPC, 30% premium budget
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar)**: New Year's Resolution buyers, solid volume
- **Summer (Jun-Aug)**: Lower local demand, but international buyers active
- **Adjust**: Reduce spend Q2/Q3, increase Q4/Q1

### Budget Allocation
- **80/20 Rule**: 80% in proven audiences/creatives, 20% in testing/new initiatives
- **Portfolio**: Diversify across Meta + Google Search + YouTube (reduce single-platform risk)
- **ROAS Tiers**: 
  - High performers (4:1+ ROAS) → Scale 20-30%/week
  - Good performers (2-3:1 ROAS) → Maintain
  - Low performers (<1.5:1) → Pause or redesign

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Underfunding audience ramp**: Launching with $1-2K/month insufficient for luxury segment
2. **Broad targeting in luxury**: "All interests in real estate" = terrible CAC
3. **No video in 2024**: Luxury buyers expect property videos, not statics
4. **Mobile-unfriendly LP**: 70% mobile traffic, 0.5% conversion rate
5. **Ignoring brand audience**: Solo focus on non-brand drives up CAC 2-3x
6. **No CRM integration**: Can't track ROI beyond platform metrics
7. **Same creative 60+ days**: Creative fatigue kills performance after 30-45 days
8. **Over-reliance on discounting**: Luxury audience doesn't respond to "50% off"
9. **Wrong attribution window**: Luxury = 60-90 day buyer journey, not 1 day
10. **Ignoring international**: 30-40% of Miami luxury buyers are international

## REAL ESTATE SPECIFIC

### Property-Level Performance Tracking
- Track which properties generate best engagement
- Highest-price units = lower volume, but higher LTV
- "Just listed" creates urgency halo effect
- "Pending" or "Sold" removes property, but can show "similar" alternatives

### International Buyer Segments
- **Brazil**: Highest volume, familiar with USD pricing, investment-focused
- **Argentina**: Ultra-luxury tier, cash buyers, private networks
- **Canada**: Tech workers, stable income, family-oriented
- **Venezuela**: Exodus money, real estate safe haven (sensitivity: vet clients)

### Luxury Positioning Cues
- Mention exclusivity: "Limited to X units", "Private community"
- Amenities matter: Smart home, wine cellar, boat dock, private elevator
- Developer reputation: "By [prestigious developer]" increases trust
- Financing: "Jumbo loans available", "International buyers welcome"
